Our Story

Built by our father, fired every night

Ember & Ash opened in 2019, when our father, Sal, finally signed a lease on Franklin Street after thirty years of baking bread in Bensonhurst. He lined the oven with brick himself. Our mother, Rosa, still makes the Sunday sauce that goes on the meatballs.

Now the three of us run the room. Dough goes in at five, the first pies land by half past, and the wine list leans on growers we have actually visited. Nine stools, one long counter, and a fire you can watch while you eat.

  • Brick oven lined by hand, burning oak at 900 degrees
  • Dough cold-fermented 48 hours, never frozen
  • Forty natural wines, all from growers we know
